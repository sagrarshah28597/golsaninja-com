Google Local Services Ads, Simplified

Google Local Services Ads (LSA) is a pay-per-lead advertising program for service businesses — plumbers, electricians, HVAC, legal, dental, and 100+ other categories. Rather than bidding for clicks, LSA charges only for real leads (calls, messages, bookings), and Google ranks providers by how fast they respond and how well-rated their leads are, not by ad spend. Listings carry the Google Guaranteed badge once a business passes licensing, insurance, and background checks.

LSANinja automates the part of LSA that determines who wins: response speed and lead quality. It answers missed calls within 30 seconds with an AI receptionist, rates and submits every lead back to Google automatically so the algorithm learns to send better-matched leads, and tracks cost-per-lead and ROI in real time — all without manual CRM work. Agencies can run the same automation across every client from one dashboard, white-labeled under their own brand.
